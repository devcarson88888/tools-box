WHY WE'RE HERE ✳

Good tools shouldn't get in your way.

Sometimes you just need to check a time zone, tidy up a block of text, or make sense of a file. Instead of wading through clutter, ads, and complicated setup, you should be able to open one clear tool and carry on.

That's the idea behind tools.box: bring handy things into one welcoming place, keep each experience focused, and make useful feel effortless.
